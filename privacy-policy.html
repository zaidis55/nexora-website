Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Nexora Agency ("we", "us", or "our") provides digital services including web development, automation, and AI-powered communication tools such as WhatsApp business messaging. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, and email address, through our website contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Messages you send us, including text and voice messages sent through WhatsApp to any business number connected to our automation services (for example, a restaurant booking assistant).
  • Booking or order details you share with us, such as reservation dates, party size, or special requests, in order to complete a request on your behalf.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and general usage patterns when you visit our website, used only to keep the site working correctly.

2. How we use WhatsApp Business messaging data

When you message a WhatsApp number that runs on our automation platform, your message content (text or voice) is processed by an AI assistant in order to respond to you, and may be transcribed automatically if sent as a voice note. Booking or request details you provide are stored (for example, in a secure spreadsheet or database) only for the purpose of fulfilling that request and are shared only with the business you are messaging.

We do not sell WhatsApp message content or contact details to third parties, and we do not use your messages for advertising purposes.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the service you requested.
  • To process and confirm bookings, orders, or requests made through automated assistants we operate for a business.
  • To improve the reliability of our websites and automation workflows.
  • To communicate with you about a project you have asked us to work on.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate — such as cloud hosting, messaging platforms (including Meta's WhatsApp Business API), and AI processing providers — solely to deliver the service you requested, and under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain contact and booking information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, respond to your enquiry, or comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. You can also stop messaging any WhatsApp assistant we operate at any time.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at hello@nexoraagency.pk or via WhatsApp at +92 92 2414 0.
